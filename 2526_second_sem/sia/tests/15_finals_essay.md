
## Essay Questions

A university needs a new enrollment system. The enrollment module requires custom features for unique scheduling policies, but payment processing is standard. The budget is $500,000 with a 12-month timeline. Analyze the build versus buy decision for each component, describe how Total Cost of Ownership should be calculated, and explain which Cost-Benefit Analysis metrics would best justify the decision.

---

A hospital's legacy patient records system from the 1990s communicates using HL7 messages over direct connections. It must be integrated with a modern cloud-based scheduling system that uses REST APIs. Identify the integration challenges, recommend middleware or integration techniques to bridge the two systems, and describe the infrastructure and platform considerations required for the solution.

---

An e-commerce platform must deploy a major update during the holiday shopping season. Compare two deployment strategies that minimize risk, recommend one with justification, and outline the post-release support structure including support tiers, monitoring, and rollback procedures that should be in place.

---

Your company is evaluating two cloud vendors. Vendor A offers 99.99% uptime with no security compliance certifications. Vendor B offers 99.5% uptime and is ISO 27001 certified. Your application handles sensitive financial data and must maintain at least 99.9% uptime. Analyze the trade-offs, recommend a vendor with supporting rationale, and specify which contract documents and SLA terms you would require to protect your organization.
